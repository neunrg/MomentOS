The problem

Relationships are the business.
Remembering them shouldn't be this hard.

A birthday wish is how a client remembers you back. But checking a diary, hunting for the right number, and sending one by one doesn't scale — and it's the first thing quietly dropped in a busy week.

Someone gets missed

Past a few dozen clients, no diary and no memory keeps up. A missed birthday is a missed chance to be remembered back.

The family gets left out

Clients want their spouse and parents greeted too. That's more numbers to track by hand — and more places for mistakes.

Manual sending doesn't scale

Typing "Happy Birthday," attaching an image, sending — one contact at a time — works for five people. Not for five hundred.

What it isn't

Being honest about the boundaries

Good tools are clear about what they deliberately don't do. So are we.

It doesn't create the greeting images. You design them however you already do — MomentOS stores, shows, and sends them.

It isn't a marketing platform. No ad blasts, no campaigns — just personal, occasion-based greetings.

It isn't a multi-business platform. One business runs its own copy — no shared cloud, no team accounts.

It doesn't send automatically — not yet. A person presses the button, by design, for a final review before anything goes out.

It doesn't touch your other chats. Nothing beyond the greeting messages it sends.
